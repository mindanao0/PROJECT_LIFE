## 1. 📐 หมวดวิทยาการคัดเลือก Pareto ขั้นสูง (Advanced Pareto Science)

### `EQ-01` [Hypervolume Indicator ($HV$ / Lebesgue Measure)]
$$HV(S, r) = \Lambda\left(\bigcup_{x \in S} \left[f_1(x), r_1\right] \times \left[f_2(x), r_2\right] \times \dots \times \left[f_M(x), r_M\right]\right)$$

### `EQ-02` [Crowding Distance Assignment (NSGA-II)]
$$I[i]_{\text{distance}} = I[i]_{\text{distance}} + \frac{f_m(I[i+1]) - f_m(I[i-1])}{f_m^{\max} - f_m^{\min}}$$

### `EQ-03` [Inverted Generational Distance Plus ($IGD^+$)]
$$IGD^+(P, P^*) = \frac{1}{|P^*|} \sum_{v \in P^*} \min_{u \in P} d^+(u, v), \quad d^+(u, v) = \sqrt{\sum_{i=1}^M \left(\max\{u_i - v_i, 0\}\right)^2}$$

### `EQ-05` [Jensen-Shannon Divergence ($JSD$)]
$$JSD(P \parallel Q) = \frac{1}{2} D_{KL}(P \parallel M) + \frac{1}{2} D_{KL}(Q \parallel M), \quad M = \frac{1}{2}(P + Q)$$

### `EQ-06` [Kolmogorov-Smirnov Non-Parametric Statistic ($D$)]
